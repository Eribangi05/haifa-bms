# Demo script (5 minutes) and preparation

For presenting Abasare to the client. The service is a working product in testing, so say that up front: MoMo, SMS, push notifications and the paid database are the known gaps and are covered in `docs/IMPROVEMENT_BACKLOG.md`.

## Before the meeting (30 minutes before)

1. **Wake the server.** On the free Render plan the first request after a quiet period takes 30 to 60 seconds. Open `https://abasare-api.onrender.com/health` until it answers `{"ok":true,...}`.
2. **Load the demo data.** Console, Administration, **Demo data**, **Load demo data** (40 drivers, 90 riders, 14 days of history is the default). It takes a few seconds. Demo data is tagged, so **Remove demo data** deletes only it.
3. **Check the console pages**: Overview, Live map (about half of the 40 demo drivers move on the map), Revenue, Ratings.
4. **Install the latest APK** on two phones: one rider, one driver. Charge both and keep mobile data as a backup to the Wi-Fi.
5. **Real driver, real rider.** Sign in on the driver phone with a real number, approve it in the console (Review queue) if it is new, and keep it **online**. Demo drivers are shown on maps but never receive trip offers, so a live trip needs the real driver phone.
6. Optional: sign in on a third screen as a demo person: rider `+250788990001` (many trips and receipts), driver `+250788991001` (a week of earnings). The code shows on screen while the test code option is on.

## The 5 minutes

| Min | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Rider phone, home map | Nearby cars are shown like Uber and Bolt (positions are blurred on purpose). "See all available drivers" widens the view across Rwanda. |
| 0:45 | Book a trip: pick a pickup place (not only "my location"), choose a destination, choose Moto or Car | Pickup is flexible: my location, search, a saved place, or a tap on the map; also booking for someone else. Fare is shown before booking. |
| 1:30 | Driver phone: offer with countdown, accept, then the rider sees the driver, plate and PIN | The trip PIN protects both sides. Chat hides phone numbers. In-app navigation with spoken directions. |
| 2:15 | Rider: **Share your trip** link, open it on the third screen | Family follows the trip without installing the app. The link expires by itself. |
| 2:45 | Complete the trip (cash), rate the driver | Ratings feed the staff view. |
| 3:15 | Rider, Trips: **Share receipt** | A link to a printable receipt (save as PDF from the browser). |
| 3:30 | Driver, Earnings: **Weekly statement**, **Share statement** | The driver shares a clear weekly statement. |
| 4:00 | Console, **Revenue** | Revenue (commission), customer spend, paid to drivers, active drivers, trend by day or week, comparison with the previous period. **Download CSV** and **Print / Save as PDF** give the client a document to take away. |
| 4:30 | Console, **Ratings** | Drivers below the limit are flagged for a coaching call; low ratings with comments. Limits are settings. |
| 4:45 | Console, **Live map**, language switch (Kinyarwanda, French, English) | One language at a time, never mixed. Close with the roadmap (MoMo, SMS, push, paid hosting). |

Extra if time allows: **scheduled ride** (booking, "Schedule for later" on the booking screen: tomorrow 06:30; the demo data has three), **airport** transfer (airport fee added to the fare), **Abasare** (hire a driver for your own car), Finance and Alerts pages.

## If something goes wrong

* **No driver found**: the real driver phone is not online or has no GPS fix. Open the driver app, go online, wait 10 seconds. Demo drivers cannot take trips.
* **The map is empty in the console**: the server was asleep. Open `/health`, reload the console, wait 20 seconds (demo drivers refresh every 20 seconds).
* **Pop-up blocked on "Print / Save as PDF"**: allow pop-ups for the console address, or use Download CSV.
* **No SMS code**: the test code option shows the code on the sign-in screen.

## After the meeting

Console, Demo data, **Remove demo data**. Real records are not touched. The Revenue and Ratings pages go back to real numbers.

## What the demo data is

Generated by `backend/src/services/demo.ts`: deterministic, about 550 to 1,000 trips over 14 days with morning and evening peaks, cash and MoMo payments (simulated), commission and ledger entries that balance, ratings (a few weaker drivers so the coaching flags have something to show), and three scheduled rides for tomorrow. It is clearly **not real business data**: do not present the figures as results.
