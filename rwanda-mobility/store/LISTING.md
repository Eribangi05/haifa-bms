## English
**Title:** Abasare: Rides in Rwanda
**Short (80):** Safe moto and car rides, plus a verified driver for your own car.
**Full:** Abasare helps you move across Rwanda. Book a moto or a car, see a clear price before you confirm, pay by cash or Mobile Money, and share your trip with people you trust. Every driver is verified. Use your trip PIN and the SOS button for extra safety. Need a driver for your own car after a night out or for a few hours? Choose Abasare: a verified driver comes to you and drives your car, with a recorded check-in and check-out of its condition. Available in Kinyarwanda, French and English.

## Français
